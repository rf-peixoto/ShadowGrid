How it works

There are two kinds of accounts. The game master runs the server and signs in as admin. Players join through an invite link, and their account only opens the game they were invited to.

A game holds its maps, its runners and the runs (contracts) the GM hands out. Everything updates live: when the GM opens a door, resolves an attack or pays out a run, players see it within a second or two without reloading.

The rules follow the Shadowrun Sixth World core rulebook: metatype limits and abilities, skills with specializations and expertise, every weapon, armor, implant, vehicle, spell, power, spirit and complex form from the book, dice pools with the Wild Die and Edge, Attack Rating against Defense Rating, ammo, drain and fading, healing, and NPCs by Professional Rating. The app does the arithmetic; the table keeps the judgment calls.

Players

For players

The top bar has everything you need during a session. Use the tabs to switch between Map, Character, Store, Runs and Wiki. On the right you always see your health, Edge, Karma and nuyen.

Join a game

  1. Open the invite link your GM sent you. Each link works once and expires after a week.
  2. Pick a username and a password of at least 8 characters, then click Create account & join.
  3. You land on your character sheet. Next time, sign in on the main page with the same username and password.

You can change your password any time from the menu with your name in the top right corner.

Build your runner

A new character starts as a draft. While it's a draft you edit freely, everything you buy is refunded in full if you return it, and the store sells anything up to Availability 6. When you're done, click Submit for approval. Your GM approves the sheet or sends it back with a note.

  • Metatype sets your attribute maximums and gives its abilities for free: elves and orks see in low light, dwarfs and trolls see heat, dwarfs resist toxins, orks and trolls are Built Tough, trolls have dermal deposits.
  • Magic / Resonance: Mundane, Magician, Aspected Magician, Adept, Mystic Adept or Technomancer. Magicians pick a tradition (Hermetic drains with Logic, Shamanic with Charisma) and may follow a mentor spirit.
  • Role is just a label, such as Street Samurai or Decker. It gives no bonuses in Sixth World.

Derived values are calculated for you: Composure, Judge Intentions, Memory, Lift/Carry, movement, Initiative (plus Matrix and astral initiative), condition monitors, wound modifiers and Defense Rating.

After approval you improve with Karma: an attribute or skill costs the new rating × 5, a specialization or expertise 5, a knowledge skill 3, a spell or complex form 5, and a positive quality twice its cost. Purchases are final. Metatype, magic type, tradition and aspect can only be changed by the GM.

Skills and knowledge

Each skill shows its rank, a specialization (+2 dice when it applies) and an expertise (+3). Expertise grows out of a specialization once the skill is at rank 5: Make expertise turns your specialization into expertise and frees the slot for a new specialization. The pool on the right is what you roll. Some skills, like Astral or Cracking, can't be used untrained.

Below the skills, add knowledge skills (no ranks: they let you use Perception or Memory on the subject) and languages at Basic, Specialist (+2), Expert (+3) or Native. One native language is free at creation.

Contacts

Contacts are how you get gear. Each contact has a Connection and a Loyalty (1-12) and a list of what they can source: weapons, armor, cyberware, magic, vehicles, and so on. A fixer can find anything. A contact can get an item whose Availability is lower than their Connection.

Store, gear and ammo

The Store tab has every item from the core book, by category: weapons, ammunition and explosives, accessories, armor, electronics, cyberdecks and software, IDs, tools, security gear, medical supplies, cyberware, bioware, magic items, vehicles and drones. Each card shows the game stats, the Availability (L means a license is needed, I means illegal) and the price. Rated items have a rating box, implants a grade (used, alphaware, betaware, deltaware), and ammo is sold in boxes of 10.

After approval, legal items up to Availability 2 are always on sale; everything else needs a contact. Untick the filter to see what a new contact would unlock: locked cards say exactly what kind of contact you'd need.

Your items appear in the Gear section of your sheet, grouped by category:

  • Equip weapons and armor. Your best body armor plus helmets, shields and implants add to your Defense Rating.
  • Reload guns from your ammo stacks. The rounds loaded and the ammo type (APDS, explosive, gel and so on) are shown and change the weapon's Attack Rating and damage.
  • Rolling an attack spends the ammo by firing mode, and throwing a grenade or throwing knife uses it up.
  • Vehicles and drones have their stats and a damage monitor. Foci are bonded from here or from the Magic section.
  • Sell gives you the game's buy-back percentage, again through a contact who could handle the item (implants included). During creation, Return refunds the full price instead.

Magic and resonance

Awakened characters get a Magic section: Magic and its maximum, spell Attack Rating, drain pool and astral initiative; spells and rituals from the book (with drain values); adept powers with levels and a Power Point counter; foci to bond; spirits; and initiation with metamagics.

  • Casting from the dice roller rolls drain right after, and applies it: Stun, or Physical when the Drain Value is higher than your Magic. Amp Up and bigger areas add to the drain.
  • Summon rolls Conjuring + Magic against the spirit; net hits are services and the spirit's hits are your drain.
  • Initiate costs 10 + the new grade in Karma, raises your Magic maximum and adds a metamagic.
  • Implants cost Essence, and every point of Essence lost lowers Magic by 1.

Technomancers get a Resonance section instead: living persona, complex forms (threading rolls fading afterwards), sprites to compile and register, and submersion with echoes.

Matrix

The Matrix section picks your active device: a cyberdeck, a commlink or, for technomancers, the living persona. It shows Attack, Sleaze, Data Processing and Firewall, Matrix Attack and Defense Ratings, Matrix initiative for AR, cold-sim or hot-sim, the device's Matrix condition monitor and your running programs. A cyberdeck takes Data Processing and Firewall from your cyberjack. The Matrix itself is played on a map.

On the map

Once the GM places your token, the Map tab shows only what your runner has seen. The fog lifts as you explore, and closed doors block your sight. Icons above tokens and enemies show their statuses, such as Prone or Burning.

  • Move: drag your token, click any room you can reach (a dotted line shows the path), or use the arrow keys.
  • Doors: the buttons in the sidebar open the doors next to you, or click a door on your own cell. Locked doors need the GM.
  • Travel: standing on a stairway or portal shows a Travel button to the next map.
  • Ping: double-click a cell to flash it for the whole table.
  • View: Isometric view switches between the tilted view and a flat one.

Dice, combat and healing

The roller is in the map sidebar and in the Combat section of your sheet. Pick a pool from your sheet: a skill (with specialization or expertise), a weapon, a spell, Defense, Damage resistance and more. Your wound modifier is applied automatically.

  • Firing modes: single shot uses 1 round; semi-auto 2 (−2 AR, +1 DV); burst 4 (−4 AR, +2 DV); full auto 10 (−6 AR).
  • Wild Die: on a 5 or 6 it counts as 3 hits; on a 1 your 5s don't count.
  • Threshold: the hits you need. The result says SUCCESS or FAILURE and shows net hits.
  • Push: spend 4 Edge before rolling to add your Edge rating, with 6s exploding. After rolling you can spend Edge on one boost: click dice to select them, then choose.
  • Initiative lets you pick physical, Matrix or astral initiative, and shows your actions: 1 Major and 1 Minor, plus 1 Minor per Initiative Die (5 Minor at most). If the GM is running combat, your score goes straight into the order.

Healing follows the book. First aid and medkits roll Biotech + Logic against 5 − the patient's Essence; a Heal spell rolls Sorcery + Magic. Each works once per set of injuries and never heals drain. You can treat yourself or an ally. Rest rolls Body + Willpower per hour for Stun and Body + Body per day for Physical, with +2 when someone trained in Biotech looks after you.

Runs and lifestyle

The Runs tab lists the contracts the GM gave you, with their tasks and progress. Tasks marked * are mandatory. When the GM closes the run, your reward is added to your nuyen.

Your lifestyle is charged by the GM at the start of each month (Street is free, Squatter 500¥, Low 2,000¥, Middle 5,000¥, High 10,000¥, Luxury 100,000¥). If you can't pay, your nuyen goes negative: you're in debt.

The Wiki tab has rules summaries, statuses, actions, and every item, spell, power, spirit, complex form, quality and NPC in the game, with a search box.

Game master

For the game master

Start a game

  1. Start the server with python app.py. The first start prints the GM password in the console. Change it later with python app.py set-password.
  2. Sign in with the username admin and that password.
  3. Click New game and give it a name. Each game has its own maps, runners and runs.

Build maps

On a game, use New map to draw one from scratch or Generate map for a procedural one. The generator offers rooms and corridors, winding passages and caves, on square or hex grids, with a live preview. The same seed always produces the same map.

In Edit mode you have these tools:

  • Paint, Rectangle and Erase draw rooms.
  • Secret hides cells from the map station; players only find them by walking in.
  • Door adds doors on cell edges.
  • Marker places enemies, loot, NPCs, objectives and portals to other maps. Pick an NPC template and new markers get that NPC's stats, weapons and condition monitor. For a quick enemy, a Body and Willpower are enough for a monitor.
  • Room names a group of cells.

Changes save automatically, and Ctrl+Z undoes them. Autosave never undoes what happened live meanwhile: doors players opened and damage or statuses from combat are kept.

Invite players

Invite player creates a single-use link that is valid for 7 days. Send one link to each player. Unused links are listed on the game page under Invite links, where you can copy or revoke them.

Approve character sheets

A submitted sheet shows a Waiting for approval tag on the home page, the game page and in the map editor. A runner with new or changed contacts shows how many are waiting for you to confirm.

  • Approve sheet locks the character into normal play, fills their Edge and confirms their contacts.
  • Send back… returns it to draft with a note the player sees.
  • As GM you can change anything on any sheet for free. Set Karma / Nuyen / Edge… sets exact values, including initiation and submersion grades.
  • Give an item… (in Gear) adds anything from the store, at any rating, grade and quantity, or a custom item. No nuyen and no contact needed.

Run the session

Switch the map editor to Play mode during the game. It opens in the isometric view; toggle it with I.

  • Viewing as: GM view shows everything. Pick a runner to see exactly what they see.
  • Reveal and Hide: paint a runner's fog with a brush, or reveal a whole room. The same buttons exist under Everyone for the whole party.
  • Doors: click to open or close, and Shift+click to lock.
  • Token: place or move the selected runner's token.
  • Markers and Select: click an enemy to open its inspector: its NPC stat block, its condition monitor, its statuses and attack buttons.
  • Statuses: under each runner in the sidebar and in a marker's inspector, add Prone, Burning, Zapped, Cover and the rest. Their icons appear on the map for everyone. Only you can set and clear them.
  • Snapshots: save and restore the whole game state (positions, exploration and doors).

NPC templates cover every grunt in the core book from Professional Rating 0 to 10, the critters, and spirits of any Force. Apply NPC template… in the inspector turns any marker into one.

Combat

The Combat panel in Play mode runs the initiative tracker. Start combat… picks the runners and enemies on this map and rolls the NPCs' initiative. Players' own initiative rolls land in the order automatically.

  • Show to players shares the order on their map. Enemies you mark Hide stay off their list until you click Reveal.
  • Each entry shows its actions (1 Major plus its Minor actions). Click a name to make it their turn and find them on the map; type a score to override it.
  • Next turn, New round, Roll NPCs, + Add (including custom combatants) and End.

Attacks are resolved by you, with every roll made by the server. Open ⚔ Attack… from the tracker, a runner or a marker:

  1. The attacker rolls their weapon pool (runners use their sheet, NPCs their template). The runner's ammo is spent.
  2. The target defends with Reaction + Intuition (Willpower + Intuition against direct spells, Reaction + Willpower against indirect ones). Ties go to the attacker.
  3. The Damage Value is the weapon's DV + net hits (+ firing mode and ammo); the target soaks with Body. Direct spells can't be soaked.
  4. The rest goes to the right monitor, Stun overflowing into Physical. A combat spell also rolls the caster's drain.

Under Modifiers you can add dice to any test, extra DV or Amp Up, override an NPC's pool, use the Wild Die, roll unopposed against weak grunts, keep the roll hidden, or roll without applying damage. The full breakdown goes to the table log.

Give out runs

On the Runs tab, New run asks for a name, a reward per runner, a description the players see, the tasks and the runners who take the job.

Tick tasks as they happen. The payout scales with the share of tasks done, and every mandatory task must be ticked before the run can be completed. Complete & pay… suggests the payout (you can change it) and pays it to each assigned runner.

Money, Karma and Edge

  • Award Karma / Nuyen: give to one runner, several or everyone. Negative amounts take it away. Each award has a reason the runner sees in their history.
  • Charge monthly lifestyle: each runner pays their lifestyle (SINner and a suprathyroid gland cost more). Nuyen can go negative.
  • Store buys at …%: what players get when they sell gear.
  • Edge: only you refresh or award Edge. Use −, + and ↻ next to each runner, or Refresh everyone's Edge. Edge from Attack Rating against Defense Rating is shown in the attack result but awarded by hand.
  • History: every Karma and nuyen change in the game.

Rules reference

Dice

  • A 5 or 6 is a hit. Glitch: more than half of the dice show 1. Critical glitch: a glitch with no hits.
  • Wild Die: on a 5 or 6 it counts as 3 hits; on a 1, none of the 5s in the roll count.
  • An untrained skill rolls the attribute −1. Specialization +2, expertise +3.

You can hold at most 7 Edge. The GM refreshes and awards it.

Combat

  • Ranges: Close 0-3 m, Near 4-50 m, Medium 51-250 m, Far 251-500 m, Extreme 500+ m. No Attack Rating at a range means the weapon can't reach.
  • Defense Rating = Body + armor (best body armor, plus helmet, shield, implants and qualities).
  • Damage after soaking higher than Body knocks the target Prone. Electricity adds Zapped, fire Burning, cold Chilled, chemicals Corrosive.
  • Actions per round: 1 Major, 1 Minor + 1 per Initiative Die (max 5). 4 Minor make a Major.

Condition monitors

  • Physical: 8 + Body ÷ 2. Stun: 8 + Willpower ÷ 2, rounded up. −1 die per 3 boxes on each track.
  • A full Stun track knocks you out, and further Stun overflows into Physical.
  • A full Physical track means dying. Overflow boxes equal Body × 2; filling them is death.

Availability

  • Character creation: up to Availability 6.
  • After approval: legal items up to 2 are always available; otherwise you need a confirmed contact who covers the goods with a Connection higher than the Availability.
  • Implant grades: used (Essence ×1.1, price ×0.5, Availability −1), alphaware (×0.8, ×1.2, +1), betaware (×0.7, ×1.5, +2), deltaware (×0.5, ×2.5, +3).
